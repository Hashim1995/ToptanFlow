# US-049: Cash reports and statements

- **ID:** US-049
- **Title:** Cash reports and statements
- **Parent Epic:** [EPIC-011](../epics/EPIC-011-cash-expenses.md)
- **Status:** Planned
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Business actor:** Controller / Cashier

## Statement

As a controller, I want Cash Account statements and initial company cash
reports, so that custody and spend are explainable.

## High-level scope

Balance by account; Total Company Cash; daily in/out; expenses by category;
transfers; partner receipts/payments; negatives; cancellations; running-balance
statement. Transfers excluded from income/expense; reversals must not inflate
turnover incorrectly.
