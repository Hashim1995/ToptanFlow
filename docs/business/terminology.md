# Business Glossary

> Source: `docs/analysis/01-document-analysis.md` (the single source of truth for this document). Definitions are business-oriented and describe what each term means to the operation of TOPTANFLOW, not how it is implemented. Where a term's precise scope is an open decision or a documented conflict in the analysis, this is noted rather than resolved. Terms marked **Obsolete** were withdrawn by Approved Human Decision (ADR-029 for warehouse/stock vocabulary; ADR-030 for dual receivable/payable primary balances; ADR-031 for Currency CRUD / multi-currency document currency in the current release) and must not be treated as active business vocabulary.

| Name | Definition | Related Modules |
|---|---|---|
| **Posting** | The act of making a transaction (sale, purchase, payment, quantity adjustment, etc.) the official business event. Only a posted transaction affects product quantity, cash, partner debt balance, cost, or profit. Posting a Sale or Purchase never itself mutates a money-account cash balance; cash effects require a separately posted Cash In / Cash Out (see ADR-028). Partner debt effects follow ADR-030. | Sales, Purchasing, Products, Cash, Settlement, Audit |
| **Draft** | A saved, not-yet-posted version of a transaction. A draft has no product-quantity, cash, partner debt balance, cost, or profit effect and can still be revised. | Sales, Purchasing, Field Sales (Yatı), Attachments |
| **Warehouse** | **Obsolete (ADR-029).** Formerly: a physical or logical location where inventory was stored and tracked separately. There is no Warehouse module; product quantity is company-wide on the Product. | — |
| **Vehicle Warehouse** | **Obsolete (ADR-029).** Formerly: a warehouse assigned to a Yatı trip/vehicle. Withdrawn with multi-location stock; Yatı awaits redesign without vehicle warehouses. | — |
| **Product Quantity (`currentQuantity`)** | The single company-wide quantity of a product currently on hand. Owned by the Product; there is no separate Warehouse or Stock module (ADR-029). | Products, Sales, Purchasing |
| **Product Quantity History** | The append-only, auditable ledger of quantity deltas for a product (purchase, sale, return, cancellation reversal, initial quantity, manual adjustment, etc.). `currentQuantity` must reconcile to the sum of these deltas. History rows are never silently edited. | Products, Audit |
| **Manual Quantity Adjustment** | An authorized correction that changes a product's quantity via a Product Quantity History row with a mandatory reason (and permission). Includes write-off-style removals at product level; not warehouse-scoped. | Products, Audit |
| **Minimum Quantity Alert** | A notification triggered when a product's `currentQuantity` crosses a defined product-level minimum threshold, requiring acknowledgment or action until resolved. Replaces warehouse-scoped critical stock alerts (ADR-029). | Alerts, Products |
| **Cash Account (Money Account)** | A separately managed pool of company money with its own current balance, transaction history, exactly one responsible User, and active/inactive status. One User may be responsible for at most one account. Responsibility supplies that logged-in user's editable operation-form default; it is not exclusive permission and does not replace operator attribution (ADR-032, ADR-040). Any number may exist as data—never as hardcoded per-person modules. Current-release amounts are AZN (ADR-031). Prefer **Cash Account** in UI; **Money Account** is a legacy synonym in older docs. | Cash |
| **Total Company Cash** | Sum of current balances of all **active** Cash Accounts. Internal transfers do not change this total (ADR-032, ADR-034). | Cash |
| **Cash Transfer (Internal)** | One atomic business operation that moves money from a source Cash Account to a destination Cash Account via linked transfer-out and transfer-in movements. Not income, not expense, no partner-debt effect (ADR-034). | Cash |
| **Expense Category** | A managed label for operating expenses (fuel, rent, utilities, …). Required on Expense Cash Out; soft-deactivate; history preserved. | Cash, Expenses |
| **Vehicle Cash Account** | The money account assigned to a specific Yatı trip that holds the starting float, field-sale proceeds, and collections, and pays trip expenses; it is kept separate from the main cash account. (Yatı redesign deferred under ADR-029; do not couple to a vehicle warehouse.) | Field Sales (Yatı), Cash |
| **Base Currency (AZN)** | The static system currency for the current release. All monetary amounts in Products, Business Partners, Sales, and Purchases are AZN. Not a CRUD entity (ADR-031). | Global / Cross-Cutting, Cash (future) |
| **Currency (module / document currency)** | **Obsolete for current release (ADR-031).** Formerly: optional multi-currency selection and Currency reference CRUD. Currency is reserved for a future Cash design only; it is not a Product, Business Partner, Purchase, or Sale property now. | — |
| **Debt Balance (signed)** | The single signed AZN debt position of a Business Partner. `>0` = partner owes the company; `<0` = company owes the partner; `=0` = no outstanding debt. Sale, purchase, cash receipt/payment, return, and cancellation effects update this one balance (ADR-030). Prefer this term over separate receivable/payable as primary truth. | Business Partners, Settlement, Sales, Purchasing, Cash |
| **Business Partner Balance Movement** | An append-only, auditable record of a change to a partner's Debt Balance (source document or authorized adjustment, delta, actor, reason). The partner's current debt balance must reconcile to these movements; manually editing the balance without a movement is forbidden (ADR-030). | Business Partners, Settlement, Audit |
| **Customer Advance** | **Not a separate primary balance (ADR-030).** Historically: money received from a customer beyond open sale amounts. In the active model, overpayment / advance is represented by the signed Debt Balance (typically when the balance is driven toward or below zero by receipts). Derived reporting labels remain optional later. | Settlement, Sales, Business Partners |
| **Supplier Advance** | **Not a separate primary balance (ADR-030).** Historically: money paid to a supplier beyond open purchase amounts. In the active model, overpayment / advance is represented by the signed Debt Balance. Derived reporting labels remain optional later. | Settlement, Purchasing, Business Partners |
| **Allocation** | Optional assignment of a cash receipt or payment to one or more sale or purchase documents for traceability, history, and reporting. Supports partial amounts and multi-document links. Does not create separate receivable/payable primary ledgers; partner debt truth is the signed Debt Balance (ADR-030). Unlinked cash remains allowed (ADR-028). | Settlement, Sales, Purchasing, Cash |
| **Reallocation** | Changing a previously made allocation, for example moving a payment from one document to another; it reopens document-level open amounts affected by the original allocation and posts corresponding reversing balance movements as required. | Settlement |
| **Settlement** | Matching money received or paid against specific sale or purchase documents (optional linkage) while the partner's overall position is the signed Debt Balance (ADR-030). | Settlement, Business Partners, Sales, Purchasing, Cash |
| **Receivable** | **Obsolete as a separate primary balance (ADR-030).** Plain language may still say a sale increases partner debt (partner owes us when balance &gt; 0). Do not maintain a separate receivable ledger as business truth. | — |
| **Payable** | **Obsolete as a separate primary balance (ADR-030).** Plain language may still say a purchase decreases partner debt (we owe the partner when balance &lt; 0). Do not maintain a separate payable ledger as business truth. | — |
| **Cost** | The value assigned to goods on hand or to a sale's cost of goods, recorded (snapshotted) from the applicable cost at the time of the relevant transaction and preserved historically even if later costs change. | Costing, Products, Sales, Purchasing |
| **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** | The recorded cost of the products delivered in a posted sale, used together with sales revenue to determine gross profit. | Costing, Sales, Reporting |
| **Weighted Average Cost** | A proposed method of valuing goods on hand by averaging the cost of all units of a product currently held. It is recommended in the business requirements but not yet formally approved as the required costing method, while the technical specification mandates it; this is a documented, unresolved point. | Costing, Products |
| **Provisional Cost / Provisional Profit** | A cost or profit figure that is marked as not-yet-final because it depends on an unresolved negative-quantity situation; it must later be adjusted once the true cost is known. | Costing, Products, Reporting |
| **Bundle** | A commercial combination of products sold together at one price, while its individual component products are deducted from product quantity and costed at sale time. | Bundles and Promotions, Sales, Products |
| **Component** | An individual product that makes up part of a bundle; a bundle's availability is limited by the least available required component. | Bundles and Promotions, Products |
| **Yatı** | The business process of field sales trips (loading goods and cash, field sales/collections, reconciliation before close). Under ADR-029, warehouse-transfer loading and vehicle warehouses are **withdrawn**; Yatı is deferred pending redesign without multi-location stock. | Field Sales (Yatı), Products, Cash, Sales, Settlement |
| **Trip (Yatı Trip)** | A single, uniquely identified field sales journey assigned to a vehicle, a Field Sales Representative, and (where applicable) a Driver. Formerly assumed its own vehicle warehouse and vehicle cash account; warehouse half is obsolete under ADR-029 pending redesign. | Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Starting Float (Starting Expense Cash)** | The initial amount of cash transferred from the main cash account to a vehicle cash account at the start of a trip, intended to cover trip expenses; it is an account transfer, not an expense. Deferred with Yatı redesign (ADR-029). | Field Sales (Yatı), Cash |
| **Old-Balance Collection** | Money collected in the field against a customer's previously posted, still-open sale, without being tied to a new sale on the same visit. Affects Cash and the partner's signed Debt Balance (ADR-030). | Field Sales (Yatı), Settlement |
| **Trip Reconciliation** | The process, performed when a trip returns, of comparing loaded, sold, returned, and remaining goods and cash for a trip against physical counts. Warehouse-scoped inventory reconciliation is **withdrawn under ADR-029**; cash reconciliation awaits Yatı redesign. | Field Sales (Yatı), Products, Cash |
| **Yellow Card** | A business-partner-specific reminder or restriction note that can display information only (INFO), require an acknowledgment/reason (CONFIRM), or prevent a transaction from posting (BLOCK) when that partner is selected. | Business Partners, Alerts |
| **Adjustment** | An approved correction to a product quantity, a cash balance, or (if supported) a partner debt balance made to align system records with a verified physical or authorized reality, always requiring a reason and, above a threshold, manager approval. Product-quantity adjustments use Product Quantity History (ADR-029); partner debt adjustments use a Business Partner Balance Movement (ADR-030). | Products, Cash, Business Partners |
| **Audit (Audit History)** | An unchangeable, append-only record of who performed a significant action, when, on which document or record, what values changed, and why; it can never be edited or deleted by any user. | Audit |
| **Period Close (Period Closing)** | The control event, typically at month-end, that locks a business period against new backdated postings, business-date changes, cancellations, and allocation changes for that period. | Audit, Settlement, Cash, Products |
| **Reopening** | An explicitly authorized, reasoned action that lifts a closed control state (a closed accounting period or, per the BRD, potentially a closed trip) so that a correction can be made, after which it must be closed again. | Audit, Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Reversal** | A compensating transaction that creates the exact opposite product-quantity, cash, or partner debt-balance effect of an original posted transaction, without deleting or editing the original. | Audit, Sales, Purchasing, Cash, Products |
| **Cancellation** | An authorized action that marks a posted document (e.g., a sale or purchase) as void and reverses its effects; the original document and its number are retained and never reused, and a corrected replacement is created separately if needed. | Sales, Purchasing, Audit |
| **Correction** | Any authorized method of fixing a mistake in a posted transaction — a return, a cancellation and reversal, a reallocation, or an approved adjustment — as opposed to directly editing the original record. | Audit, Sales, Purchasing, Settlement, Products, Cash |
| **Sale** | A document recording the delivery of goods to a business partner, including price and discounts (AZN); posting a sale decreases product quantity and increases the partner's Debt Balance by the sale amount. No warehouse or currency selection. It never directly changes a money-account balance; cash settlement uses separately posted, optionally linked cash receipts (ADR-028, ADR-029, ADR-030, ADR-031). | Sales, Products, Settlement |
| **Sales Return** | A document recording that all or part of a previously sold product has been returned by a customer, decreasing the partner's Debt Balance by the reversed sale amount and restoring resaleable product quantity at the original sale's cost (ADR-030). | Sales, Products, Settlement |
| **Purchase** | A document recording goods actually received from a supplier, including actual quantity and price (AZN); posting a purchase increases product quantity and decreases the partner's Debt Balance by the purchase amount. No warehouse or currency selection. It never directly changes a money-account balance; cash settlement uses separately posted, optionally linked supplier payments (ADR-028, ADR-029, ADR-030, ADR-031). | Purchasing, Products, Settlement |
| **Purchase Return** | A document recording that all or part of a previously received product is being returned to a supplier, increasing the partner's Debt Balance by the reversed purchase amount and decreasing product quantity using the original purchase cost where possible (ADR-030). | Purchasing, Products, Settlement |
| **Cash In** | Primary Cash operation: money enters a selected **Cash Account** from a **Business Partner**. Increases the account balance and decreases the partner's signed Debt Balance by the same amount (ADR-030, ADR-038). Optional link to a Sale is for traceability only. Not a separate “Customer Receipt” product. | Cash, Settlement, Sales |
| **Cash Out** | Primary Cash operation: money leaves a selected **Cash Account** paid to a **Business Partner**. Decreases the account balance and increases the partner's signed Debt Balance by the same amount (ADR-030, ADR-038). Optional link to a Purchase is for traceability only. Not a separate “Supplier Payment” product. | Cash, Settlement, Purchasing |
| **Cash Transfer (Internal)** | One atomic business operation moving money between two Cash Accounts (source ≠ target). Produces linked TRANSFER_OUT + TRANSFER_IN; Total Company Cash unchanged; neither income nor expense; no partner debt (ADR-034, ADR-038). | Cash |
| **Customer Receipt / Supplier Payment** | Legacy clarifying aliases for partner Cash In / Cash Out. Must not appear as separate primary navigation or menu features (ADR-038 / CHANGE-005). | Cash (deprecated primary UX names) |
| **Stock Count** | **Obsolete as a warehouse process (ADR-029).** Formerly: physically counting a warehouse's inventory. Any future physical quantity check must operate at product level via Manual Quantity Adjustment / Product Quantity History, not a Warehouse Stock Count module. | — |
| **Write-Off** | An approved posting that removes damaged, lost, obsolete, or expired goods from product quantity with a mandatory reason and evidence where required. Product-level (not warehouse-scoped); recorded via Product Quantity History (ADR-029). | Products |
| **Negative Stock (Controlled Negative Quantity)** | A temporary, visible, and permission-controlled exception in which a product's `currentQuantity` goes below zero because goods are believed to physically exist while their receipt has not yet been recorded; it is not treated as normal available quantity. Blocked without permission; allowed with permission + mandatory reason (ADR-029; ADR-025 v1: all active users have permission). Full BRD-OD-04 case machinery remains deferred. | Products, Costing |
| **Negative Cash (Controlled Negative Cash)** | A temporary, visible, controlled exception in which a Cash Account's balance goes below zero because an urgent payment was authorized ahead of its funding. Default blocked; override requires mandatory reason and stored before/after balances (ADR-037). Amount/age/case limits remain open. Must never be concealed by a false receipt. | Cash |
| **Cash Closing** | The process of comparing a Cash Account's calculated (system) balance to its physically counted balance at a defined point (end of day/shift), investigating any difference, and recording an approved adjustment if the difference cannot be explained. | Cash |
| **Expense** | Primary Cash operation: operating outflow from a Cash Account (fuel, rent, utilities, salary, etc.) with a required Expense Category and description. Decreases cash only — **no** Business Partner field and **no** partner debt change (ADR-038). | Expenses, Cash |

| **Personal Financing (Personal Funder)** | A situation in which an employee or the owner pays a business expense using personal funds; the amount is tracked as a separately reimbursable obligation rather than as a business cash outflow. | Expenses, Cash |
| **Fixed Asset** | A long-lived operational asset (such as a vehicle or equipment) used by the business over time, tracked separately from Products and product quantity, with its own acquisition, custody, maintenance, and disposal history (ADR-029). | Fixed Assets |
| **Business Partner** | A single record representing a person or company that can act as a customer, a supplier, or both, with one signed Debt Balance (ADR-030). | Business Partners |
| **Customer** | A business partner who receives goods, invoices, refunds, statements, or messages and may have a positive Debt Balance (partner owes us) or other signed position after receipts. | Business Partners, Sales |
| **Supplier** | A business partner who supplies goods, assets, or services, receives payments, refunds purchase returns, and may have a negative Debt Balance (we owe the partner) or other signed position after payments. | Business Partners, Purchasing |
| **Aging** | Classification of outstanding partner debt (by sign and/or document linkage) by how long amounts have been outstanding relative to due date. Primary truth remains the signed Debt Balance (ADR-030). | Settlement, Reporting |
| **Business Partner Statement** | A read-only, chronological listing of a partner's sales, returns, purchases, receipts, payments, and adjustments over a date range, showing one running signed Debt Balance (opening through closing) (ADR-030). | Business Partners, Settlement, Reporting |
| **WhatsApp Sharing** | The user-controlled act of sending a posted invoice or a partner statement to a business partner through WhatsApp for informational delivery; sending or reading such a message is never treated as payment confirmation. | Messaging and Document Sharing, Business Partners |
| **Attachment (Evidence)** | A photograph or document captured and linked to a business event (receipt, expense, trip, return, quantity adjustment, or asset); it supports but never itself posts or approves a transaction. | Attachments and Mobile Evidence |
| **Critical Stock Alert** | **Superseded naming (ADR-029).** Prefer **Minimum Quantity Alert**. Formerly evaluated at main-warehouse / specific-warehouse / company-total levels; now only the product-level minimum quantity threshold applies. | Alerts, Products |
| **Idempotency** (business meaning) | The requirement that resubmitting the same request (for example after a network failure or retry) must not create a duplicate business effect, such as a duplicate posted sale or duplicate payment. | Global / Cross-Cutting |
| **Opening Balance / Cutover** | The approved starting position (product quantities, cash, and partner signed debt balances) recorded when the business begins using TOPTANFLOW, entered as a distinct migration action rather than as ordinary sales, purchases, receipts, or payments. Opening quantity is per product, not per warehouse (ADR-029, ADR-030). | Migration and Opening Balances |
| **Business Date** | The date on which a transaction is considered to have occurred for business and reporting purposes, which may need to be distinguished from the date it was actually recorded in the system. | Global / Cross-Cutting, Reporting |
| **Approval** | A required, attributable authorization by an appropriately permissioned user (typically a Manager or Business Owner) for a defined high-risk action, such as a large discount, a product-quantity adjustment, a negative-balance exception, or a period reopening. | Users & Permissions, Audit |
| **Role / Permission** | A named collection of authorized actions assigned to a user; viewing information and performing a transaction are always separate, individually grantable permissions. | Identity, Users, Roles, and Permissions |
| **Maker/Approver Separation** | An optional business policy under which the person who creates a document must be different from the person who approves or posts it. | Users & Permissions, Audit |
| **Product Category** | A reusable flat classification label for products, maintained as reference data independently of product type (finished good / raw material / mixed-use); products may optionally reference one active category. Historical category attribution on posted documents remains an open reporting decision (BRD-CA-18). | Products |
| **Product Code / Business Partner Code** | A backend-generated, immutable, human-readable business reference for a Product or BusinessPartner (independent sequential decimal text, initially seven-digit zero-padded; never reused after deactivation). Distinct from the technical UUID primary key. Clients do not supply or override these codes (ADR-024). | Products, Business Partners |

| **Gross Profit** | Sales revenue (net of returns) minus the recorded cost of goods sold, excluding taxes and other operating expenses. | Reporting, Costing |
