# Business Glossary

> Source: `docs/analysis/01-document-analysis.md` (the single source of truth for this document). Definitions are business-oriented and describe what each term means to the operation of TOPTANFLOW, not how it is implemented. Where a term's precise scope is an open decision or a documented conflict in the analysis, this is noted rather than resolved.

| Name | Definition | Related Modules |
|---|---|---|
| **Posting** | The act of making a transaction (sale, purchase, payment, transfer, count, etc.) the official business event. Only a posted transaction affects inventory, cash, receivables/payables, cost, or profit. | Sales, Purchasing, Inventory, Cash, Settlement, Audit |
| **Draft** | A saved, not-yet-posted version of a transaction. A draft has no inventory, cash, receivable, payable, cost, or profit effect and can still be revised. | Sales, Purchasing, Field Sales (Yatı), Attachments |
| **Warehouse** | A physical or logical location where a company's inventory is stored and tracked separately from other locations. | Inventory, Sales, Purchasing |
| **Vehicle Warehouse** | A warehouse assigned to a specific Yatı trip/vehicle that holds goods transferred from the main warehouse for field sale; it remains company inventory and is consolidated into company totals. | Field Sales (Yatı), Inventory |
| **Money Account** | A tracked store of company or vehicle cash (main cash, bank, or vehicle cash) against which receipts, payments, expenses, and transfers are recorded. | Cash, Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Vehicle Cash Account** | The money account assigned to a specific Yatı trip that holds the starting float, field-sale proceeds, and collections, and pays trip expenses; it is kept separate from the main cash account. | Field Sales (Yatı), Cash |
| **Customer Advance** | Money received from a customer that exceeds the open receivable amount it was allocated against; it is neither revenue nor a settled receivable until applied to a future document. | Settlement, Sales, Business Partners |
| **Supplier Advance** | Money paid to a supplier that exceeds the open payable amount it was allocated against; it is neither an expense nor a settled payable until applied to a future document. | Settlement, Purchasing, Business Partners |
| **Allocation** | The act of assigning a specific payment or receipt amount to one or more specific open sale or purchase documents, reducing their open (unpaid) amount. | Settlement, Sales, Purchasing, Cash |
| **Reallocation** | Changing a previously made allocation, for example moving a payment from one document to another; it reopens the balances affected by the original allocation. | Settlement |
| **Settlement** | The overall process of matching money received or paid against specific sale or purchase documents, tracking what remains open (receivable/payable) by partner and by document. | Settlement, Business Partners, Sales, Purchasing, Cash |
| **Receivable** | An amount a business partner (customer) owes the company, created when a sale is posted and reduced by allocated payments, returns, or approved cancellations. | Settlement, Sales, Business Partners |
| **Payable** | An amount the company owes a business partner (supplier), created when a purchase is posted and reduced by allocated payments, returns, or approved cancellations. | Settlement, Purchasing, Business Partners |
| **Cost** | The value assigned to inventory or to a sale's cost of goods, recorded (snapshotted) from the applicable cost at the time of the relevant transaction and preserved historically even if later costs change. | Costing, Inventory, Sales, Purchasing |
| **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** | The recorded cost of the products delivered in a posted sale, used together with sales revenue to determine gross profit. | Costing, Sales, Reporting |
| **Weighted Average Cost** | A proposed method of valuing inventory by averaging the cost of all units of a product currently held. It is recommended in the business requirements but not yet formally approved as the required costing method, while the technical specification mandates it; this is a documented, unresolved point. | Costing, Inventory |
| **Provisional Cost / Provisional Profit** | A cost or profit figure that is marked as not-yet-final because it depends on an unresolved negative-stock situation; it must later be adjusted once the true cost is known. | Costing, Inventory, Reporting |
| **Bundle** | A commercial combination of products sold together at one price, while its individual component products are deducted from inventory and costed at sale time. | Bundles and Promotions, Sales, Inventory |
| **Component** | An individual product that makes up part of a bundle; a bundle's availability is limited by the least available required component. | Bundles and Promotions, Inventory |
| **Yatı** | The business process of loading goods and starting cash onto a vehicle for a field sales trip, recording field sales and collections against that trip, and reconciling remaining inventory and cash before the trip closes. | Field Sales (Yatı), Inventory, Cash, Sales, Settlement |
| **Trip (Yatı Trip)** | A single, uniquely identified field sales journey assigned to a vehicle, a Field Sales Representative, and (where applicable) a Driver, with its own vehicle warehouse and vehicle cash account. | Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Starting Float (Starting Expense Cash)** | The initial amount of cash transferred from the main cash account to a vehicle cash account at the start of a trip, intended to cover trip expenses; it is an account transfer, not an expense. | Field Sales (Yatı), Cash |
| **Old-Balance Collection** | Money collected in the field against a customer's previously posted, still-open sale, without being tied to a new sale on the same visit. | Field Sales (Yatı), Settlement |
| **Trip Reconciliation** | The process, performed when a trip returns, of comparing loaded, sold, returned, and remaining inventory and cash for a trip against physical counts, investigating any differences, and resolving or approving them before the trip can close. | Field Sales (Yatı), Inventory, Cash |
| **Yellow Card** | A business-partner-specific reminder or restriction note that can display information only (INFO), require an acknowledgment/reason (CONFIRM), or prevent a transaction from posting (BLOCK) when that partner is selected. | Business Partners, Alerts |
| **Adjustment** | An approved correction to an inventory quantity or a cash balance made to align system records with a verified physical reality (e.g., a stock count difference or a cash-closing difference), always requiring a reason and, above a threshold, manager approval. | Inventory, Cash |
| **Audit (Audit History)** | An unchangeable, append-only record of who performed a significant action, when, on which document or record, what values changed, and why; it can never be edited or deleted by any user. | Audit |
| **Period Close (Period Closing)** | The control event, typically at month-end, that locks a business period against new backdated postings, business-date changes, cancellations, and allocation changes for that period. | Audit, Settlement, Cash, Inventory |
| **Reopening** | An explicitly authorized, reasoned action that lifts a closed control state (a closed accounting period or, per the BRD, potentially a closed trip) so that a correction can be made, after which it must be closed again. | Audit, Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Reversal** | A compensating transaction that creates the exact opposite inventory, cash, or receivable/payable effect of an original posted transaction, without deleting or editing the original. | Audit, Sales, Purchasing, Cash, Inventory |
| **Cancellation** | An authorized action that marks a posted document (e.g., a sale or purchase) as void and reverses its effects; the original document and its number are retained and never reused, and a corrected replacement is created separately if needed. | Sales, Purchasing, Audit |
| **Correction** | Any authorized method of fixing a mistake in a posted transaction — a return, a cancellation and reversal, a reallocation, or an approved adjustment — as opposed to directly editing the original record. | Audit, Sales, Purchasing, Settlement, Inventory, Cash |
| **Sale** | A document recording the delivery of goods to a business partner, including price, discounts, and payment status; posting a sale reduces inventory and creates a receivable for any unpaid amount. | Sales, Inventory, Settlement |
| **Sales Return** | A document recording that all or part of a previously sold product has been returned by a customer, reducing the open receivable first and restoring resaleable stock at the original sale's cost. | Sales, Inventory, Settlement |
| **Purchase** | A document recording goods actually received from a supplier, including actual quantity, price, and payment status; posting a purchase increases inventory and creates a payable for any unpaid amount. | Purchasing, Inventory, Settlement |
| **Purchase Return** | A document recording that all or part of a previously received product is being returned to a supplier, reducing the open payable first and decreasing inventory using the original purchase cost where possible. | Purchasing, Inventory, Settlement |
| **Inventory Transfer** | A movement of stock between two warehouses (including central-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-central movements) that changes location only, not company-total quantity or value. | Inventory, Field Sales (Yatı) |
| **Stock Count** | A process of physically counting a warehouse's inventory and comparing it to system quantities, producing variances that require a reason and, above a threshold, approval before posting. | Inventory |
| **Write-Off** | An approved posting that removes damaged, lost, obsolete, or expired inventory from stock with a mandatory reason and evidence where required. | Inventory |
| **Negative Stock (Controlled Negative Inventory)** | A temporary, visible, and permission-controlled exception in which a warehouse's recorded stock for a product goes below zero because goods are believed to physically exist while their receipt has not yet been recorded; it is not treated as normal available stock. | Inventory, Costing |
| **Negative Cash (Controlled Negative Cash)** | A temporary, visible, and permission-controlled exception in which a money account's balance goes below zero because an urgent payment was authorized ahead of its funding; it must be resolved by a later real receipt or funding, never concealed by a false receipt. | Cash |
| **Cash Closing** | The process of comparing a money account's calculated (system) balance to its physically counted balance at a defined point (end of day/shift), investigating any difference, and recording an approved adjustment if the difference cannot be explained. | Cash |
| **Expense** | A recorded business operating outflow (such as fuel, rent, or supplies) that is not a purchase of sellable inventory and does not need to be linked to a sales or purchase document. | Expenses |
| **Personal Financing (Personal Funder)** | A situation in which an employee or the owner pays a business expense using personal funds; the amount is tracked as a separately reimbursable obligation rather than as a business cash outflow. | Expenses, Cash |
| **Fixed Asset** | A long-lived operational asset (such as a vehicle or equipment) used by the business over time, tracked separately from sellable inventory, with its own acquisition, custody, maintenance, and disposal history. | Fixed Assets |
| **Business Partner** | A single record representing a person or company that can act as a customer, a supplier, or both, while its receivable and payable balances remain tracked separately. | Business Partners |
| **Customer** | A business partner who receives goods, invoices, refunds, statements, or messages and may owe a receivable or hold an advance. | Business Partners, Sales |
| **Supplier** | A business partner who supplies goods, assets, or services, receives payments, refunds purchase returns, and may hold or be owed an advance. | Business Partners, Purchasing |
| **Aging** | The classification of open receivable or payable amounts by how long they have been outstanding relative to their due date, used to identify overdue balances. | Settlement, Reporting |
| **Business Partner Statement** | A read-only, chronological listing of a partner's sales, returns, purchases, receipts, payments, advances, and adjustments over a date range, showing opening and closing balances separately by direction. | Business Partners, Settlement, Reporting |
| **WhatsApp Sharing** | The user-controlled act of sending a posted invoice or a partner statement to a business partner through WhatsApp for informational delivery; sending or reading such a message is never treated as payment confirmation. | Messaging and Document Sharing, Business Partners |
| **Attachment (Evidence)** | A photograph or document captured and linked to a business event (receipt, expense, trip, return, count, or asset); it supports but never itself posts or approves a transaction. | Attachments and Mobile Evidence |
| **Critical Stock Alert** | A notification triggered when a product's stock crosses a defined minimum threshold at the main-warehouse, specific-warehouse, or company-total level, requiring acknowledgment or action until resolved. | Alerts, Inventory |
| **Idempotency** (business meaning) | The requirement that resubmitting the same request (for example after a network failure or retry) must not create a duplicate business effect, such as a duplicate posted sale or duplicate payment. | Global / Cross-Cutting |
| **Opening Balance / Cutover** | The approved starting position (inventory, cash, receivables, payables, and advances) recorded when the business begins using TOPTANFLOW, entered as a distinct migration action rather than as ordinary sales, purchases, receipts, or payments. | Migration and Opening Balances |
| **Business Date** | The date on which a transaction is considered to have occurred for business and reporting purposes, which may need to be distinguished from the date it was actually recorded in the system. | Global / Cross-Cutting, Reporting |
| **Approval** | A required, attributable authorization by an appropriately permissioned user (typically a Manager or Business Owner) for a defined high-risk action, such as a large discount, an inventory adjustment, a negative-balance exception, or a period reopening. | Users & Permissions, Audit |
| **Role / Permission** | A named collection of authorized actions assigned to a user; viewing information and performing a transaction are always separate, individually grantable permissions. | Identity, Users, Roles, and Permissions |
| **Maker/Approver Separation** | An optional business policy under which the person who creates a document must be different from the person who approves or posts it. | Users & Permissions, Audit |
| **Product Code / Business Partner Code** | A backend-generated, immutable, human-readable business reference for a Product or BusinessPartner (independent sequential decimal text, initially seven-digit zero-padded; never reused after deactivation). Distinct from the technical UUID primary key. Clients do not supply or override these codes (ADR-024). | Products, Business Partners |

| **Gross Profit** | Sales revenue (net of returns) minus the recorded cost of goods sold, excluding taxes and other operating expenses. | Reporting, Costing |
